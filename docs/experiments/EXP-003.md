# EXP-003 — SAHI sliced inference (COCO128, no retrain)

**Status:** Concluded (two-branch compare: smoke weights vs 896-trained weights).

**Question:** Can **tiling (SAHI)** mimic high-resolution behavior without retraining at large `imgsz`?

**What we did:** SAHI on fixed val GT vs vanilla `predict` for the **same** checkpoints; compare JSONs vs EXP-000 and vs EXP-002b **896**.

**Conclusion (recorded):** SAHI on **320-trained** weights raises overall mAP and mAP_medium vs vanilla 320, but **mAP_small stayed flat**. Against **vanilla 896 on 896-trained weights**, SAHI is **worse** on headline mAP and area buckets—**tiling does not replace training at the target resolution** here. FPS comparisons across vanilla vs SAHI benchmark modes need cautious wording.

**Paper role:** Justifies **full-frame inference at chosen `imgsz`** for later EXP-A005 / Camponotus work unless tiling is revisited with domain-specific justification (Table S2: background).
